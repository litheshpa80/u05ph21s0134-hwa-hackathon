Try it yourself

Can Your Pup Crack It?

A mini Treat Vault, right here in your browser. Watch the scent trail, slide the gates in the same order, and uncover the hidden treat.

How to play

  1. Watch the trail. The three gates flash open in a secret order.
  2. Repeat the order. Slide each gate with a tap. Right gates unlock; wrong ones just wiggle.
  3. Crack the vault. Open all three to pop the hidden treat.

🐾Wrong gate? No stress — pups learn by sniffing around. Tap Watch the trail again for a hint.
